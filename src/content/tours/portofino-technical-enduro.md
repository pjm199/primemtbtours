## Pure Enduro Experience

This is the tour for experienced riders seeking adrenaline and technical challenges. Navigate steep descents, rock gardens, and rooty trails through the stunning Portofino Natural Park.

### What Makes This Special

- **Technical terrain**: Advanced trails with rocks, roots, and drops
- **Epic descents**: Long downhill sections through forest
- **Expert guidance**: Professional enduro guides
- **Small groups**: Maximum 6 riders
- **Full suspension bikes**: High-end enduro bikes included

### The Challenge

Expect steep climbs rewarded with thrilling descents. Technical rock gardens, tight switchbacks, and natural obstacles will test your skills. This tour is designed for riders with advanced mountain biking experience.

### Fitness Required

Good fitness level required. You should be comfortable on technical terrain and capable of riding for 6 hours with breaks.

### What's Included

- Professional enduro guide
- High-end full suspension bike
- Full-face helmet option
- Protective gear available
- Lunch and refreshments
- Shuttle service for key climbs

### Prerequisites

- Advanced MTB skills required
- Previous technical trail experience
- Good physical fitness
- Minimum age: 18 years
