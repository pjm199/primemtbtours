## Tour Highlights

Experience the stunning beauty of the Cinque Terre coastline on this gentle, beginner-friendly mountain bike tour. This 4-hour adventure takes you along scenic coastal paths with breathtaking views of the Mediterranean Sea.

### What to Expect

- **Easy riding**: Suitable for beginners and families
- **Stunning views**: Panoramic vistas of the Italian Riviera
- **Photo opportunities**: Multiple stops at scenic viewpoints
- **E-bike available**: Electric bike option for easier riding
- **Small groups**: Maximum 8 riders for personalized experience

### Tour Details

The route follows well-maintained coastal trails with minimal elevation gain. We'll ride through charming villages, past terraced vineyards, and along cliff-side paths with spectacular ocean views.

Perfect for those who want to enjoy the beauty of the Cinque Terre region without intense technical riding.

### What's Included

- Professional local guide
- Quality mountain bike or e-bike
- Helmet and safety equipment
- Water and snacks
- Photo stops at best viewpoints

### Meeting Point

We meet at our shop in Levanto at 9:00 AM. Free parking available nearby.
